Story Studio privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Story Studio is a private tool used by one person, its owner, to manage their own Instagram and YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, given through Google's and Meta's own sign-in screens, Story Studio reads, for the owner's own channels only:

  • the list of the channel's videos and their public counts (views, likes, comments);
  • the channel's analytics (for example watch time and how long viewers stay);
  • comments on the owner's videos, to count viewers' votes.

If the owner turns posting on, Story Studio also uploads the owner's own finished videos to the owner's own channel, only when the owner presses Send.

How it uses the data

Only to show the owner how their videos performed and to publish the owner's videos. Commenters are stored as a short anonymous fingerprint, not by name.

Where the data is kept

On the owner's own computer. Sign-in keys are kept in the computer's protected keychain. Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to anyone except Google and Meta, and only to make the requests above.

Google user data

Story Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Story Studio uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

How long it is kept, and how to remove it

Numbers are kept until the owner deletes them. Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's security settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or Meta's Business Integrations settings; Story Studio then can no longer read or post anything.
