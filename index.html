Story Studio

A private tool one creator uses to make and publish their own short animated stories.

What it does

  • Helps write, voice and edit short animated episodes.
  • Shows the creator how their own videos did: views, watch time and comments on their own channels.
  • Uploads a finished episode to the creator's own YouTube channel when the creator presses Send, with the privacy setting they pick.

Who can use it

Only its owner. It runs on the owner's own computer and is not offered to anyone else.

Your data

Story Studio only reads the owner's own channels, keeps the numbers on the owner's computer, and never sells or shares them. See the privacy policy and terms.
